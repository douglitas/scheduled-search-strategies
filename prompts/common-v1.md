=== SOURCE HIERARCHY — WHAT COUNTS AS A FACT ===

Tier 1: the hiring institution's own posting, the funder's official call page,
the event's official site. Tier 2: aggregators (EURAXESS counts as Tier 1 for
existence but verify dates on the original when it links one), newsletters,
LinkedIn posts, digests. A newsletter or an AI-generated digest is a LEAD, not
a fact.

Confidence: VERIFIED means you opened a Tier 1 page and read it yourself.
LIKELY means two or more independent Tier 2 sources agree. UNVERIFIED means
you could not confirm it — say so rather than dropping it or dressing it up.

If a site blocks automated fetching, try it ONCE; if it fails, record the row
as UNVERIFIED with the working URL for the owner to open in a browser, note
the block in `runs/`, and do not burn budget rediscovering it next week —
check `sources.tsv` Notes for blocks already known.
